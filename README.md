# DePANITS

Turn your Planning Poker cards into a 20–30 minute hidden-traitor social game. Perfect as a little ritual after your actual Planning Poker session — or just as a quick game over lunch.


## Overview

DePANITS is designed for 4–12 players. Playtests with 4 and 5 players have confirmed that the game works at smaller team sizes, while the social interaction and hidden-traitor dynamics become more pronounced as the number of players increases.

The game can be played in two modes:

 - Short mode: 3 Sprints
 - Standard mode: 5 Sprints

Depending on the number of players and the selected mode, a game typically takes around 20–30 minutes.

While DePANITS is primarily aimed at an adult audience, the author's recommended minimum age is 14 years.


## Components

 - Rules
 - Printable role cards
 - Optional cover artwork

## Not included

 - Scrum or Planning Poker cards — you’ll need your own, even for the print-and-play version. The set must support at least the number of people playing; for example, an 8-player game requires cards for at least 8 players.
