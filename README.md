# DePANITS

Turn your Planning Poker cards into a 15–30 minute hidden-traitor social game. Perfect as a little ritual after your actual Planning Poker session — or just as a quick game over lunch.


## Components

 - Rules
 - Printable role cards
 
 
## Not included

 - Scrum or Planning Poker cards — you’ll need your own, even for the print-and-play version
